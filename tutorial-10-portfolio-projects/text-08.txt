Exercise: Record yourself (voice or video) answering these questions.
Then review and critique your answers.

Questions to practice:

1. "Walk me through one of your DevOps projects from start to finish."
   Target: 3-4 minutes. Cover: problem, solution, tools chosen, outcome.

2. "What's the most important engineering decision you made in that project?"
   Target: 2 minutes. Must include WHY, not just WHAT.

3. "What would you do differently if you built it again?"
   Target: 90 seconds. Shows growth mindset and critical thinking.

4. "How do you handle a production outage at 3am?"
   Target: 2 minutes. Walk through your incident response process.

5. "Explain Kubernetes to someone who has never heard of it."
   Target: 90 seconds. No jargon. Use an analogy.

Self-evaluation criteria:
(yes) Did you mention specific tools by name?
(yes) Did you explain WHY you chose those tools?
(yes) Did you mention what could go wrong and how you'd handle it?
(yes) Could a non-technical person understand the value?
(yes) Did you stay under the target time?

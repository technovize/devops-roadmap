Template: CKA 12-Week Study Schedule

Week 1-2: Core Concepts
Daily: 30 min video + 90 min hands-on lab + 15 min practice questions
Topics: Pods, Deployments, Services, Namespaces, ConfigMaps, Secrets
Lab: KodeKloud Kubernetes for Beginners labs (free)

Week 3-4: Workloads & Scheduling
Daily: 30 min video + 90 min hands-on lab + 15 min practice questions
Topics: Resource limits, DaemonSets, StatefulSets, Jobs, CronJobs
Topics: Node affinity, taints, tolerations, pod priority

Week 5-6: Cluster Architecture & Administration
Daily: 30 min video + 90 min hands-on lab + 15 min practice questions
Topics: kubeadm installation, etcd backup/restore, cluster upgrades
Topics: RBAC, service accounts, certificate signing requests
Lab: Build a cluster from scratch with kubeadm (use Vagrant or EC2)

Week 7-8: Networking
Daily: 30 min video + 90 min hands-on lab + 15 min practice questions
Topics: CNI plugins, NetworkPolicy, Services deep dive, Ingress
Topics: CoreDNS, kube-proxy, pod networking
Lab: Implement NetworkPolicies from scratch

Week 9-10: Storage
Daily: 30 min video + 90 min hands-on lab + 15 min practice questions
Topics: PersistentVolumes, PVCs, StorageClasses
Topics: Volume types, access modes, reclaim policies

Week 11: Troubleshooting (THE MOST IMPORTANT WEEK)
Daily: 2 hours — break things, fix them, repeat
Scenarios:
- Kill the kube-scheduler, diagnose, and fix
- Corrupt the etcd data directory, restore from backup
- Break a NetworkPolicy, debug connection failures
- Deploy a bad image, observe OOMKill, fix resource limits

Week 12: Full exam simulations
Day 1-2: Run killer.sh exam 1 (included with registration)
Day 3: Review all incorrect answers
Day 4-5: Run killer.sh exam 2
Day 6: Light review of weak areas
Day 7: REST — do not study the day before the exam

Scoring target: 80%+ on killer.sh before scheduling the real exam

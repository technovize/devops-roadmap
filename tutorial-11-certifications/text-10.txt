The AWS exam often presents two correct-looking answers where:
- Option A is correct but complex
- Option B is simpler and also correct

Rule: AWS exams prefer the SIMPLEST solution that meets ALL stated requirements.
If the question says "minimum operational overhead" -> choose the managed service
If the question says "most cost-effective" -> eliminate services with high per-unit cost
If the question says "least disruption" -> choose the option with no downtime

Common traps:
- "Multi-AZ" sounds good but adds cost — only use if "high availability" is stated
- "Reserved Instances" sounds good but not if workload is unpredictable
- "Lambda" is not always right for long-running, stateful, or high-memory workloads

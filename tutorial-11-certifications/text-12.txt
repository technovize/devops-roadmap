CKA includes one free retake (included in the $395 fee)
Use your retake wisely:

1. Review your score report: which domains did you fail?
   Focus all retake study on those specific domains

2. Wait at least 2 weeks before retaking — study genuinely
   Do NOT retake immediately after failing — you will fail again

3. Run killer.sh again — focus on tasks you got wrong the first time

4. Identify speed bottlenecks: were you too slow or did you not know the answer?
   - Too slow -> Practice the specific command sequences 50 times
   - Didn't know -> Study the specific topic with hands-on labs

For AWS exams: no free retake, but you can retake after 14 days
Schedule a retake within the year to maintain momentum

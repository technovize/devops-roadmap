Before exam day (do this 48 hours before):
1. Run the system compatibility check: https://syscheck.bridge.psiexams.com/
2. Test your webcam in the exam browser
3. Check your internet speed: speedtest.net (need at least 2 Mbps upload)
4. Prepare your government ID
5. Clear your desk completely (proctors WILL ask to see it)

Day of exam:
- Start system check 30 minutes early
- Close ALL other applications (proctors can see your screen)
- Use ethernet cable if available (more reliable than WiFi)
- Have a backup phone as a hotspot in case internet drops
- If connection drops during exam: close and reopen the browser
  The exam saves progress — you won't lose completed tasks
